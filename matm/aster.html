Aster

"The stars have a plan. I just follow where they point."

📖 Backstory

Standing eight feet tall with the head of a hippopotamus, Aster is impossible to miss. A Giff from the Astral Sea, he found himself on the Material Plane through circumstances he describes as "cosmically hilarious."

Unlike most of his kind, Aster rejected the martial traditions of the Giff in favor of druidic magic, reading meaning in the constellations and drawing power from starlight. He still carries muskets—old habits die hard—but he's just as likely to summon vines or call lightning.

Semaj immediately recognized his astral heritage, and the two share a bond born of the stars.

🧠 Psychological Profile

Aster is not easy to read, and that is by design — not by intention exactly, but by nature. Giff are not subtle people and neither is Aster, who carries a musket to a dungeon crawl and once ripped an entire weapon rack out of a wall because removing individual weapons seemed inefficient. But what the campaign keeps surfacing is a genuine and deep investment in the biology of the world. The herbs, the potion-brewing, the Speak With Animals, the Crail's Maggot that devoured Venomfang from the inside out — all of it is the same curiosity about what living things are made of, applied consistently across contexts.

The apples deserve mention. She regards one with focused, slightly proprietary attention. She harvested herbs on the road to Thundertree with the concentration of a field surgeon. She brewed a healing potion at camp over a simmering pot, carefully, without rushing. She described her Shillelagh as a golf swing and immediately committed to the metaphor — which tells you something about how she processes novelty. She finds the closest known framework and holds it while she works out what is actually in front of her. She is not a flexible thinker in the moment, but she is a thorough one.

The maggot. In the middle of a fight with a dragon, with Valrex paralyzed on the ground and the party burning through resources, Aster summoned a conjured larva inside Venomfang's chest cavity and then — when the dragon died — ran up to the body, reached inside, felt around until she found it, and ate it. When asked why, the implied answer was: it came from a dragon, it was interesting, it was available. This is not impulsivity. It is the natural endpoint of sustained curiosity. Aster finishes what she starts.
