Session XVII

The Dragon's Heart Whispers

📅 June 2026 ⏱ ~3.5 hours 👥 5 players

The party harvested Venomfang's corpse like professionals — 13 liters of blood, 43 pounds of meat, wings worth 6,000gp, two horns, four sets of scales, and the dragon's breath pouch, all extracted through a gauntlet of escalating survival checks. Then Valrex went for the heart. He needed a 25. He got a 23. In the moments after the failure, something settled behind his eyes that wasn't entirely his — Venomfang's pride and spite, whispering from where Valrex's faith used to be. It manifested almost immediately: he walked north toward the dragon's tower, claiming its horde before he realized he'd said 'my.' Then the dragon cultists arrived in green and black robes, Valrex's next Guiding Bolt fired the wrong color, and the session ended with his hand around the cult leader's throat and three nat 20s on the enemy's initiative.

Best Moments

Reidoth's Verdict

Roleplay

After staring at Venomfang's corpse for a long moment, Reidoth let out a low, quiet whistle. The old druid — who had spent years watching everything in Thundertree fail and die — looked at the party and said the four words that completed the arc: "I'll be damned." Then, practically: "If you're set on carving him up, best do it before the scavengers catch the scent." He'd believed in them just enough to fight. Now he believed all the way.

"I'll be damned. You actually did it. If you're set on carving him up, best do it before the scavengers catch the scent."

Belden Gets Both Horns

Epic

Sean made clear he intends to use one as an instrument.

The Breath Pouch — Last Chance

Tension

DC 20 to extract the dragon's breath pouch without rupturing it. Failure would destroy everything harvested and nearly kill the harvesters. Valrex had +5 survival. First roll: failed. Used inspiration: failed. Used his last lucky point: failed. Belden's inspiration as a proxy: failed. Final lifeline: Kaelia's Freya's Grace — a reaction spell that let Valrex reroll the check as a Charisma save with her +7 modifier. The roll: 16 + 7 + d4. It worked. The party exhaled as one.

"Oh. Oh. No."

— Jen/Kaelia

"You take the full weight of this in your hands."

— Alex/GM

Valrex Fails the Heart

Tragedy

The dragon's heart required a DC 25. Valrex was out of inspirations, out of lucky points, had only guidance. He went for it anyway. He rolled a 1. With guidance: still a 6. Total: 23. Two short. The heart released its residual magic. Tunnel vision — sickly green. His hands clenched involuntarily. A flicker of contempt at the weaker people near him. A possessive curl toward the dragon parts. "These are yours." The seed of Venomfang's pride and malice, lodged where his faith should be. The dragon is dead. But its heart whispers now.

"There's this flicker of contempt. When you look at these weaker people near you, this momentary thrill. A possessive curl of fingers. It's not constant. Not yet. But it's there. The seed of Venomfang's pride and malice now lodged where your faith should be."

— Alex/GM

"I've just been someone who definitely has an addictive personality. So this is the best role I've played in the entire game."

— Darren/Valrex

My Dragon Horde

Comedy

The first symptom hit almost immediately. Valrex stood covered in blood and viscera, staring north at the tower, and said — out loud, before his conscious mind caught up — "There's a dragon horde up there. My dragon horde." Then started walking. Didn't notice no one followed. Semaj reached into his mind via Message: "Snap out of it." Valrex turned back, confused. "Does it mean we're not going to get the gold? It's going to be our gold."

"You almost hear yourself saying this. There's a dragon horde up there. My dragon horde. Before you even realize you said it."

— Alex/GM

"It has begun. I think we have time. Though we can't wait too long. Otherwise, we'll have to kill him in his sleep."

— Richard/Semaj

The Cultists Arrive — Faith Draining in Real Time

Epic

They came in green and black robes expecting to serve a living Venomfang. Instead they found the skull detached and staring, the wings cut free, bones in organized piles, and a dwarf with a freshly-hollowed horn. You could see the faith drain out of them in real time. Their leader walked forward with bared teeth: "What have you done?" Valrex answered by throwing a Guiding Bolt — which fired sickly green instead of radiant. The curse had changed the color of his divine magic.

"What have you done?"

— Alex/GM (Fabric)

"It is a sickly green light. As this radiant energy just rips across this square, you see it glittering now in a green, which used to be a different color."

— Alex/GM

"Well, there goes diplomacy."

— Richard/Semaj

Inflict Wounds on Fabric — Session Cliffhanger

Chaos

Fabric walked up to Valrex to begin the ritual transfer. He asked, directly: "Are you willing to give it to me?" The word "give" landed wrong in the cursed mind. The dragon's possessiveness reacted. Valrex's hand shot out. He seized Fabric by the throat. Necrotic energy spiderwebbed into the cult leader's skin — still tinted green. Fifteen damage. The cultists pulled swords. Three nat 20s on initiative. Session ended here. Next time: July 25th.

"Your hand just shoots out. And you seize Fabric by the throat. Fingers digging into his flesh. The dark, crackling energy from your grip starts to spiderweb into his skin like lightning. Fifteen points of necrotic energy tearing through him."
