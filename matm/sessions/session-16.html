Session XVI

Venomfang Falls

📅 April 2026 ⏱ ~4 hours 👥 5 players
⬆ Level Up! → Level 4

The party stepped through Reidoth's door and into the best session of the campaign. Inside the druid's fortified cottage, Reidoth briefed the party over needle tea on the full threat picture: Venomfang's poison breath and pride, the ash zombies, the giant spiders, and the dragon cultists to the east. Semaj persuaded Reidoth to join the fight. Reidoth stocked the party with five healing potions and a potion of growth, then led them out to clear the spider nest first. Valrex immediately went rogue—wading solo into the webbed building while the rest of the party was still getting their orders. Belden answered the chaos by playing Take Me On (A-ha) on his horn. Two giant spiders poured out. The fight turned brutal: Valrex missed nearly every roll, failed concentration twice, self-inflicted Inflict Wounds, went down to poison, and fought the entire encounter paralyzed on the floor. Meanwhile, Venomfang heard the horn, left his roost, and dove toward the ruins at 50 feet up. Semaj cracked him with a Lightning Chromatic Orb from the bushes—the dragon locked eyes on the party. Kaelia hit him with Scorching Ray for 22 damage. Aster planted Crail's Maggot in the dragon's chest and let it eat from the inside. Aster's Water Bullet sent Venomfang crashing to the ground. The maggot finished the job. Party leveled to 4. Belden started harvesting the dragon horn before anyone suggested it.

Best Moments

Tea with Reidoth

Roleplay

Reidoth welcomed the party inside, warned them about muddy boots, and served needle tea with mint and chamomile. Larry told Reidoth everything — Aster cast Speak with Animals and learned that Larry had vouched for every member of the party, telling the druid they don't run from fights and that he trusts them. The old druid looked at the group with markedly more respect after that.

"Hot water at least reminds us that we're alive."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"I see. Yes, very good."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"Good boy, Larry. Good boy."

— Richard/Semaj

Semaj Persuades the Druid

Roleplay

Reidoth laid out a compelling case for why he should stay behind. Semaj didn't let him finish. With Valrex casting Guidance and Belden calling it, Semaj rolled a 25 persuasion — delivered in character — reminding Reidoth that a man who dies in his sleep leaves nothing behind. Reidoth went quiet, looked at each party member in turn, and said 'very well.'

"Everything that you've offered to us up to this point, we humbly thank you for. However, as you mentioned yourself, you are not young and spry anymore. With our group, we're your best chance of ending this threat with any sort of finality."

— Richard/Semaj

"You fight rough and loud and with more heart than sense. But you fight for something. That's more than I can say for the cultists or for the dragon. Very well. I will fight with you."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"Don't go putting that foolishness on me. But as one more set of eyes and one more voice telling you when you're about to do something stupid. Like blowing that horn."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

Belden answered by playing Take Me On on his horn. Reidoth put his hands over his face and said 'at least I died old and not stupid.'

Venomfang Arrives

Epic

Mid-spider-fight, Reidoth's head snapped up. The horn. Of course it was the horn. Wings cracked the air. Venomfang left his roost and circled in from the north at 50 feet altitude. He dove on Belden — the little trumpet man — exhaling a full cone of poison breath. Belden's dwarven resilience and a successful save cut it to 9 damage. Then Semaj hit Venomfang with a Lightning Chromatic Orb from the bushes. The dragon noticed.

"Well, here's what your horn has done now, you idiot!"

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"The little gnats bite."

— Alex/GM (Venomfang)

"Now listen to my horn."

— Alex/GM (Venomfang)

Crail's Maggot Kills a Dragon

Epic

Aster planted Crail's Maggot inside Venomfang while the dragon was airborne. The maggot gnawed through blood vessels and organs, wriggling into the dragon's heart. Venomfang crashed to the ground, lurching, wings failing, blood leaking from his mouth and scales. The killing blow came from inside. After the session ended, Aster ran up to the corpse, reached into the dragon's gut, pulled out the six-inch maggot — swollen from eating a dragon — and ate it.

"You see this grotesque lump kind of sliding inside his scales along his flank, bulging outward as this maggot gnaws its way into his heart."

— Alex/GM

"Can I use the rest of my turn to find the maggot and slurp it up?"

— Darren/Valrex

"It bursts on your tongue — somewhere between overripe fruit and a raw oyster, spilling out this hot metallic slurry taste of venom-tinged blood and half-digested tissue."

— Alex/GM

Funny

He never made it outside. The only party member who didn't fight Venomfang was also the only one who went down.

"I've rolled more ones tonight than I've rolled since I started playing D&D."

After four hours and two simultaneous fights — one against giant spiders, one against a young green dragon — the GM called it. The party had killed Venomfang and cleared the spider nest. Everyone leveled to 4. Belden started harvesting the dragon's horn before anyone suggested it. Alex promised a full dragon harvesting table next session.
