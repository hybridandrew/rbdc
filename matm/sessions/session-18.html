Session XVIII

Hammer Time

📅 July 2026 ⏱ ~2.5 hours (late start) 👥 5 players

One session, one battle — the full cultist fight from the initiative rolls that ended session 17 to the last skull caved in. Favric opened by force-choking Valrex with Hold Person and preaching that the curse belonged to him; Beldan answered by cleaving him through the back mid-sermon and finishing him with a warhammer. Kaelia burned the battlefield with Dragon's Breath and Mantle of Flame, Semaj threw an illusionary dragon roar so convincing the cultists forgot they were in a fight, Aster walked bodily through a cultist with Krail's Rupture, and Larry ripped out a throat to a chorus of 'good boy.' Five enemies died. One cultist fled into the woods carrying everything the cult now knows about the party — and the ritual to move Venomfang's curse may have died with Favric.

Best Moments

The Disarm Heard Round Thundertree

Comedy

A cultist's critical fumble sent his scimitar flying out of his hand and into the dirt behind him. Beldan — greataxe on his back, options everywhere — spent his entire turn sprinting to the fallen sword, picking it up, locking eyes with its owner, and hurling it forty feet into the woods without breaking eye contact. The cultist's morale never recovered; he spent the rest of the fight running for his weapon and eventually just fled the battle entirely.

"I want to look this cultist in the eyes, and then turn around and throw his sword as hard as I can the opposite direction."

— Sean/Belden

"You eye contact the whole time as you just sling it right into the woods. You see the cultist kind of looking around with nothing to attack with."

— Alex/GM

Favric's Sermon, Interrupted

Epic

Favric spent the fight preaching instead of fleeing — force-choking Valrex with Hold Person, promising that the curse was a blessing he had bled for, then trying to turn the party against their own cleric. He was mid-monologue, concentrating on a second Hold Person, when Beldan came through his back with a greataxe cleave, followed with a warhammer to the skull, and a Hill Strike to finish. The cult leader died at Valrex's feet — and used his last breath on a prophecy instead of a plea.

"Even your rage is his rage. You cannot carry that hate without it eating you alive. Let it pass to me, cleric. I was born for this burden."

— Alex/GM (as Favric)

"You will kill them all."

— Alex/GM (as Favric)

The Illusionary Roar

Epic

With Favric dead and the cultists rattled, Semaj cast Minor Illusion and threw a dragon's roar behind the enemy line — betting that dragon cultists, of all people, could not ignore that sound. A 22 deception check later, every cultist on the field spun around scanning the sky, and the GM handed the entire party free opportunity attacks. Kaelia — a self-described range fighter — used hers to crack a cultist's skull with her staff, her first melee kill of the campaign.

"This illusionary roar just echoes through Thundertree like a tidal wave of sound. Their eyes go wide for a heartbeat as they're now scanning the sky, and you can see the fear in their eyes."

— Alex/GM

"I'm a range fighter, you guys."

— Jen/Kaelia

"I'm just a kill stealer."

— Jen/Kaelia

Aster Walks Through a Man

Chaos

Surrounded and bored of it, Aster cast Krail's Rupture — magically entering a cultist's body and exiting through the opposite side, dealing 18 necrotic damage on the way through. Two opportunity attacks whiffed as the giff simply relocated through a human being. Earlier he'd tried diplomacy mid-combat, casting Pacify Person at Favric and accidentally pacifying Valrex instead — until the dragon's rage in Valrex's blood shattered the calm from the inside.

"The sneaky hippo just moved out of all of their attack range and passed magically through one of them."

— Alex/GM

"Valrex locks eyes with Favric. Pure rage pours out, and he screams: it's my soul now to keep."

— Darren/Valrex

Larry the Best Boy

Epic

Larry spent the fight dodging scimitars, dragging cultists prone with Pack Tactics, and — after Semaj's second-level Magic Missile softened his target — lunging at a distracted cultist's back and ripping out his throat like a dog with a chew toy. Reidoth kept him standing with Healing Word and Cure Wounds, and got a pat on the head for it. The table consensus was unanimous and repeated: good boy. Best boy.

"Larry latches onto this cultist's neck and drags him back to the ground, tearing back and forth like a dog with a chew toy."

— Alex/GM

"Larry's such a good boy. He's the best boy."

— Alex/GM

Hammer Time, Twice

Epic

Beldan's stat line for the night: three kills. Favric via cleave-hammer-Hill Strike. A second cultist smashed beside the head as he 'casually walked by.' And the last cultist standing — already frightened by Beldan's two-fingers-to-the-eyes intimidation after watching him kill his friend — died to a raised hammer and a sucker punch that drove his nose into his brain, Roadhouse style, peeing himself in his final moments. Beldan announced 'hammer time' before both hammer kills. Nobody stopped him. Nobody could.

"I believe it is hammer time."

— Sean/Belden

"Run away from me. I dare you."

— Sean/Belden

Friendly Fire (Get It?)

Comedy

Kaelia rolled a nat 1 on Sorcerous Burst and shot Reidoth square in the back. The old druid looked back mid-battle and forgave her — 'in the heat of battle, sometimes friendly fire happens.' Jen doubled down with the pun, the table groaned, and the DM took one point of psychic damage for a bad joke. Kaelia immediately converted two sorcery points via Quickened Spell and burned the correct target with the do-over. Reidoth's review: 'That's the right target.'

"Friendly fire! Get it? Cuz I got it."

— Jen/Kaelia

"The DM takes one point of psychic damage for a bad joke."

— Richard/Semaj

"That's the right target."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)
