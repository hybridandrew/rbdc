Session XV

Into Thundertree

📅 March 2026 ⏱ ~3 hours 👥 5 players

After wrapping up business in Phandalin at the Lion Shield Coster, the party loaded Gundren's wagon and began the two-and-a-half-day journey to Thundertree. Aster foraged healing potion ingredients along the way. Camp nights brought Semaj deep into meditation on the mystery of Ruxithid, and a quiet fireside conversation with Kaelia confirmed they are both tuned to the same unsettling frequency. Upon arrival at the ruins, Belden announced the party with a horn blast—summoning a swarm of twig blights. The blights were destroyed with fire, mace, and one memorable improvised horn kill. Belden then found a buried merchant's chest worth a small fortune. A cautious sweep of the ruins revealed a lone fortified house with smoke rising from the chimney. The man who answered the door introduced himself by warning them about a young green dragon called Venomfang in the tower above. The druid Reidoth had been waiting for someone brave enough—or foolish enough—to come. Session ended as he waved the party inside.

Best Moments

The Lion Shield Deal

Plot

Linene Graywind of the Lion Shield Coster recognized the party and asked about her stolen crates. Valrex rolled an 18 history check, remembered exactly where the goods were in both the Cragmaw hideout and Tresendar Manor, and relayed it to the group. Kaelia stepped in with a persuasion pitch after Valrex fumbled his negotiation, and Linene offered a discount on future purchases—including plate armor—if the party brought back a real load of stolen stock.

"I won't gut my own books. But if you're the ones who actually put my stolen stock back under my roof, that's worth more than talk. Bring me back a decent load of the Lion's Shield crates—more than a token of effort—and I will knock off a solid chunk of the price that is set for you. Call it an investment."

— Alex/GM (Linene)

"Let us go before she changes her mind."

— Richard/Semaj

The Ruxithid Trance

Roleplay

During the first night's watch, Semaj sank into deep meditation focusing on the name Ruxithid. The name felt less like a memory and more like a scar—patient, vast, alien. It bore the same sideways wrongness as the creature in Glasstaff's workshop. Kaelia's history check confirmed it matched the psychic fingerprint of aberrant powers that play very long games. The two Astral Drifters quietly confirmed to each other that something old was watching.

"Whatever Ruxithid is, it's patient. It doesn't try to seize you. It's watching. It's waiting."

— Alex/GM

"I feel like there's something connected with not only the name of the entity and what's going on in the region, but that we may cross paths with it in the future."

— Richard/Semaj

"Whatever it is—a mage, a patron, a fiend—it has some kind of aberrant power. These entities lean in from outside the usual cosmology and play very long games."

— Alex/GM

Belden Announces the Party

Funny

Approaching Thundertree, Belden blew his horn to announce their arrival at each ruined cottage. The effect was immediate: twig blights swarmed out of the brush. When the dust settled, Belden used the horn as an improvised weapon—scooping a twig blight up with the open end and blasting it out in a shower of wooden splinters. Alex awarded inspiration on the spot.

"You blow this horn. The twig blights hearing this sound are all locked eyes and fixated on you. The sound seems to agitate them."

— Alex/GM

"I take my horn with the open side down and just smoosh it all the way until the horn touches the ground. Then I lift it back up and blow into it."

— Sean/Belden

"You hear this really muffled horn blast, and from the end of the horn you see this barrage of wooden bits fly out as this twig blight gets disintegrated. Belden, take an inspiration for that."

— Alex/GM

"I have seen Sean do more improvised attacks in this campaign than I have seen anyone do in any game."

— Darren/Valrex

Belden's Nat 20 Discovery

Epic

While the party searched the ruins of Thundertree, Belden rolled a natural 20 on his investigation check and found a flagstone that didn't sit right. Beneath it was an old merchant's chest, rotted but intact, tangled in tree roots. Inside: 1,400 copper, 160 silver, and 90 gold pieces—enough to convert to 33 platinum, 9 gold, and 5 copper.

"One of the old flagstones near the eastern wall doesn't quite sit right. There's an edge that's a little more exposed. Beneath it, tangled in the roots, you see the corner of an old wooden chest—swollen and stained from years underground, but intact."

— Alex/GM

"I'm rich. I'm rich!"

— Sean/Belden

"You should give that to the treasury."

— Richard/Semaj

Meeting Reidoth

Plot

One structure in the ruins stood apart—intact roof, reinforced shutters, firewood stacked neatly outside, and herbal smoke curling from the chimney. The wiry old man in a green-brown cloak who answered the door wasted no time: a young green dragon named Venomfang had claimed the tower, and he had been waiting for someone brave enough to come deal with it.

"Travelers don't usually wander into Thundertree by accident. State your business."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"There's a green dragon. Young, but not so young that it should be underestimated. It calls itself Venomfang. It claimed the old tower and doesn't much care what else lives or dies around it. I've been watching it, learning its habits, waiting for someone brave enough or stupid enough to come."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"We are mere travelers on a quest. What is your business here?"

— Richard/Semaj

"My business is making sure this ruin doesn't spawn more trouble than it already has."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

Kaelia vs the Twig Blights

Combat

Kaelia activated Mantle of Flame the moment twig blights swarmed, then opened with Sorcerer's Burst—dealing 18 fire damage to a creature with 4 HP. When a second blight ran at her legs, it missed both times, apparently deterred by the flames. Being a fire sorcerer against plant monsters has its advantages.

"This whole creature just goes up into flame. It lets out a hiss as it collapses in a shower of burning splinters. That would have been 18 damage. It had four health."

— Alex/GM

"I really like things that are wood."

— Jen/Kaelia

"As the flames seem to have deterred them from making contact, both attacks miss."

— Alex/GM
