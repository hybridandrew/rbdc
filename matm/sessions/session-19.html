Session XIX

Hew

📅 August 2026 ⏱ ~3 hours 👥 5 players

No dice-rolling bloodbath this time — a full role-playing session where the consequences got louder than any combat. Valrex walked off alone and wordlessly claimed Venomfang's horde, and that night the curse taught him to count coins: 'Mine. Count every piece. Trust no hand.' Beldan pulled a rusty dwarven battleaxe from under the gold — Hew, +1, maximum damage against anything wooden, forged with a built-in grudge against druids — and by morning the town square's warrior statue lay decapitated with axe marks all over it ('Do you have any idea how much noise it would make for me to cut that down?'). Semaj's astral trance finally cracked the Ruxithid mystery open: Ruxithid the Chosen, a goblin altered beyond its kind by a jagged crystal in its skull. Reidoth gave the party the curse's real options — Wish, or true death and resurrection — pointed them at Cragmaw Castle's ruined library, and said goodbye. The session ended inside the castle: four waking hobgoblins, initiative rolled, King Grol arguing with a silky-voiced woman one room over, and Gundren's ragged breathing on the floor between them.

Best Moments

My Horde, Claimed

Tragedy

The moment combat ended, Valrex started walking toward the tower without a word to anyone — the curse convinced him the cultist attack had been a diversion so someone could steal his horde. He found Venomfang's lair (dead spiders mauled outside the door), a broken chest of gold, four silver goblets with moonstones, and a rusty dwarven axe — and took every piece of it. That night, sorting coins alone in the barracks, the whisper came: 'Mine.' They will call it treasure. They'll call it party spoils. Count every piece. Trust no hand.

"They will call it treasure. They'll call it party spoils. They will take what they believe they deserve. But you know better. You bled for this. Count every piece. Trust no hand."

— Alex/GM (as the curse)

"At any point before when we started this campaign, did y'all ever think that PvE would turn into PvP?"

— Darren/Valrex

Hew

Comedy

Under the horde's gold: a rusty battleaxe with 'HEW' in dwarven runes. Aster's Detect Magic read it clean — +1 weapon, steady transmutation aura, maximum damage against plants, wood, and anything wooden, built by a dwarf with a documented grudge against druids ('You should have known he was Druish'). Beldan claimed it, immediately felt an unnatural urge to fell trees, and dropped three of them before Reidoth intervened. The Neverwinter Wood has suffered enough.

"You should have known he was Druish."

— Richard/Semaj

"Belden, stop! That axe may be useful against goblins and cultists and dragons, but these trees are not enemies."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"Not gonna lie, this is kind of therapeutic."

— Sean/Belden

The Statue Did Not Chop Itself

Comedy

Morning in the town square: the ten-foot wooden warrior statue that had watched over Thundertree for generations lay in splintered lumber, head several feet from the base, axe marks everywhere. Beldan — who had spent his entire night watch 'admiring the statue' with a whetstone and Hew in hand — mounted the least convincing defense in campaign history. Kaelia responded by setting the severed head's hair on fire. Belden liked this. Belden will remember this.

"You just couldn't help yourself, could you?"

— Richard/Semaj

"Do you have any idea how much noise it would make for me to cut that down?"

— Sean/Belden

"Belden likes this. Belden will remember this."

— Sean/Belden

Ruxithid the Chosen

Revelation

Semaj's focused astral trance finally broke the campaign's deepest mystery open. An 18 insight pulled fractured images: a jagged crystal embedded in a skull, spilling outward like needles, surrounded by goblins with elongated heads — something CHOSE Ruxithid and altered him beyond his kind. Kaelia's history check named him: Ruxithid the Chosen, tied to a goblin clan and to rumors of goblins with strange gemstones that let them hear gods in their minds. Not proof of divine favor — vessels being used. The psionic tyrant has a body, and it used to be a goblin.

"A jagged crystal embedded in a skull, spilling outwards like needles — and around this skull are goblins who do not look like your normal goblins. Something has chosen him and has altered him beyond his kind."

— Alex/GM

"Why am I hearing the voice? Why am I being selected?"

— Richard/Semaj

Reidoth's Farewell

Roleplay

The old druid gave the party everything he had left: the truth about the curse (indulging it strengthens it; denying it forever may be worse), the real cures (a Wish — or true death, long enough for the dragon's echo to lose its hold, followed by resurrection that might not work), and a destination — Cragmaw Castle's halls once held a library of the region's arcane histories, and what the goblins didn't destroy may hold answers. Then he clasped forearms with Semaj, bowed, and walked back into the ruins he intends to heal.

"Keep your eyes open. Keep your friend close. And do not let the dead dragon decide the shape of your future."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

"Death may break the bond. Not simply falling unconscious. True death. But that is not a cure I would recommend lightly."

— Alex/GM (Reidoth)

The Website Assist

Meta

Nobody at the table could remember how Droop said to find Cragmaw Castle — until the party's own campaign site got pulled up mid-session and delivered the goods: three claw marks on trees, stacked stones, torn red cloth at the forks, goblins watching from the towers, and a hidden servant's door at the back wall. Every sign checked out in play, and the back entrance is exactly how the party got inside.

"Look at that website coming in handy. That's exactly pulled from the website."

— Alex/GM

Four Sleeping Hobgoblins (Cliffhanger)

Tension

Inside the castle's collapsed northeast tower, Aster's ear against a door caught the whole picture: King Grol arguing with a calm, silky-voiced woman about the dwarf, the map, and the Spider's orders — with someone's weak, uneven breathing underneath it. Gundren. Alive, badly beaten, one room away. The plan: slit four sleeping hobgoblins' throats simultaneously. The execution: Beldan's boot found the one loose stone in Cragmaw Castle. Initiative rolled — Semaj rolled a 3 and goes dead last. Session over. Beldan pre-declared his opening move for next time: toss a tree branch onto a hobgoblin so Hew gets max damage. The GM's ruling: 'You're playing the game.'

"You take the dwarf. You take the map. But Grol gets paid. The Spider sends nothing but orders."

— Alex/GM (as King Grol)

"The Spider will reward you when Gundren has told us everything he knows. You are being paid to hold him, King Grol."

— Alex/GM (as the woman)

"Well, it was a great idea, guys. It's been nice knowing you."

— Jen/Kaelia
