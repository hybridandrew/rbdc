Kaelia

📖 Backstory

Kaelia's hair glows with living flame, a constant reminder of the phoenix fire that burns within her soul. Born to a human blacksmith mother and an elven ranger father, she never quite fit in either world—too fiery for the elves, too magical for the humans.

The phoenix sorcery manifested violently in her youth, and she has spent years learning to control the flames that threaten to consume everything she touches. When she's angry, nearby fires flare. When she's frightened, sparks dance at her fingertips.

Despite her volatile nature, Kaelia has a protective streak a mile wide. She may burn hot, but that fire is aimed squarely at those who threaten the innocent.

🧠 Psychological Profile

Kaelia grew up in the space between two worlds and trusted by neither — too incandescent for elven composure, too strange for human normalcy, her phoenix fire a liability that took years to become anything close to a gift. The lesson her early life taught was not to hide, exactly, but to wait. To learn the room before offering herself to it. Her backstory describes the violence of uncontrolled manifestation, sparks at her fingertips when frightened, fires flaring when she is angry — but what the transcripts show is a woman who has converted that instinct for concealment into something close to tactical patience. She waits. She reads. She acts when she has information, not when she has feeling.

That restraint is not distance. In session one she is already manipulating the flames in the Driftwood Tavern's chandelier without gesture or word, a private act of self-regulation that no one sees. In the same session, she takes Semaj's upside-down map, turns it around in his hands without comment, and returns it to him. Both are the same move: she sees something that needs correcting, corrects it without ceremony, and moves on. By session three, the flicker in her eyes when Belden insults Semaj is the first on-screen expression of emotion the character allows. It is pointed at someone else's comfort. That is characteristic. The fire, when it shows, is almost always outward-facing.

She confirmed this herself at the pre-Thundertree campfire. Asked how much she trusted the group, she said "75% of the way there" — which for Kaelia is nearly a declaration of love. What that 75% looks like in practice was the Decelerate reaction during the Glasstaff chase, a mid-flight intercept of a Magic Missile aimed at Aster, executed without being asked, without announcement, without asking for acknowledgment afterward. That is not strategy. That is reflex. The trust had already finished forming in the body before the conscious mind had caught up to naming it.

What she shared with Semaj at the campfire — that she had heard Ruxithid too — was offered in the precise way someone offers a thing they have been holding alone for a long time: carefully, in stages, watching for evidence that the other person will not ruin it. Both of them drew a circle around the name and agreed it was larger than what they could see. Kaelia plays information the way she plays fire: not by accumulating it but by knowing exactly when to release it and where to point it. The walls she built are still there. They have simply stopped facing inward.

✨ Features & Traits

Mantle of Flame
Wreathe yourself in fire. Creatures that hit you in melee take fire damage.
Phoenix Spark
When reduced to 0 HP, explode in flames and return with half HP (1/long rest).
Control Flames
Manipulate nonmagical fire within 60 feet.
Fey Ancestry
Advantage on saves against being charmed; magic can't put you to sleep.
Elemental Adept — Fire
Her fire spells ignore resistance. Any 1 rolled on a fire damage die is treated as a 2.
Metamagic: Quickened Spell
Spend 2 Sorcery Points to cast a spell as a Bonus Action instead of an Action.
Metamagic: Seeking Spell
Spend 1 Sorcery Point to reroll a missed spell attack and use the new result.

  • Freya's Grace in Session 17: let Valrex reroll the failed DC 20 breath-pouch extraction as a Charisma save with her +7 — the lifeline that saved the entire harvest from detonation.

  • Scored her first melee kill of the campaign: staff of defense to the back of a distracted cultist's head. Her own review: "I'm a range fighter, you guys."
  • Rolled a nat 1 on Sorcerous Burst and shot Reidoth square in the back, made the friendly-fire pun anyway, then Quickened a second Burst for the make-up kill. Reidoth: "That's the right target."
