Kaelia

📖 Backstory

Kaelia's hair glows with living flame, a constant reminder of the phoenix fire that burns within her soul. Born to a human blacksmith father and an elven mother, she never quite fit in either world—too fiery for the elves, too magical for the humans.

The phoenix sorcery manifested violently in her youth, and she has spent years learning to control the flames that threaten to consume everything she touches. When she's angry, nearby fires flare. When she's frightened, sparks dance at her fingertips.

Despite her volatile nature, Kaelia has a protective streak a mile wide. She may burn hot, but that fire is aimed squarely at those who threaten the innocent.

🧠 Psychological Profile

If you did not know what to look for, you might mistake Kaelia for the group's most consistent voice of reason — and you would not be wrong, exactly. She tracks exits, monitors spell slots, reads the room before opening her mouth. But what actually drives Kaelia is not caution. It is a very particular relationship with fire: not as a tool, not as metaphor, but as the most honest version of herself she has found. When the Mantle of Flame is active, she is not performing. She is present in a way she cannot always manage otherwise.

She spent the early campaign behind a careful set of walls. She answered questions with questions, kept her past close, and watched the rest of the group for signs of rot before deciding to trust any of them. By her own accounting — delivered in a pre-Thundertree campfire conversation — she was "75% of the way" to trusting the group when Glass Staff died, and the movement from suspicious to fiercely protective was fast once it happened. She used her Decelerate reaction to stop a Magic Missile aimed at Aster during the Glass Staff chase without being asked. That is what 75% looks like in practice.

What she revealed to Semaj on that campfire watch outside Thundertree was not an accident. She has heard the name Ruxithid too. She did not blurt it out — she waited to see what he would offer first, then confirmed carefully, in stages, and both of them understood immediately that they were drawing a circle around something neither could fully see. Kaelia plays information the same way she plays combat: with patience, with precision, and with a clear sense of when to commit.

Her humor is understated enough to miss. She manipulates a candle flame in Reidoth's cottage toward Belden while maintaining perfect composure. She phrases her most pointed observations as mild observations. She is the only party member whose emotional register has not visibly shifted from the outside. But she is not the same person she was in session one. The walls are still there. They are just no longer facing inward.

⭐ Memorable Moments

  • Eyes flickered with visible fire in Session 1 when Belden insulted Semaj one too many times — her first on-screen expression of emotion.
  • Got swept away by both flood traps in Cragmaw Cave, then executed the final fleeing goblin with a firebolt to the back of the head.
  • Twin-spelled Scorching Ray to incinerate Glasstaff at dawn in Session 13 — her player's description: 'The fire robs him of his breath before it robs him of his life.'
  • Cast Dragon's Breath on Belden in Session 11, making the dwarf a fire-breathing machine — and was 'unusually happy about the amount of fire.'
  • Recognized the Lion Shield Coster symbol from Cragmaw Cave in Session 14, making the connection that cracked open the stolen-cargo quest.
  • Used Decelerate as a reaction to stop a Magic Missile mid-flight during the Glass Staff chase — saving Aster without being asked, without a word.
  • Used Prestidigitation to flood Glass Staff's workshop with a rotten egg smell, flushing out the invisible creature lurking in the corner.
  • Read the Thundertree ruins from fifty yards away and spotted Reidoth's cottage by its patched shingles, cleared brush, and chimney smoke — before anyone else had a theory.
  • Ignited Mantle of Flame in the twig blight ambush and stood untouched while the blights literally couldn't grab her — then one-shotted one with a Sorcerous Burst for 18 damage.
  • Cast Silvery Barbs to force Venomfang to reroll his save, then followed with Ancestral Communion and a flaming line spell in the same turn.
