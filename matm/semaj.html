📖 Backstory

Born into the noble Dra'hcir family among the Astral Elves, Semaj grew up surrounded by luxury and privilege. His family once held great influence, their name spoken with reverence across the planes. But fortune is fickle, and through a series of political machinations and unfortunate deals, the Dra'hcir name fell into disgrace.

Now Semaj walks the Material Plane, seeking to restore his family's honor through deeds worthy of legend. His draconic bloodline—a gift inherited through a lineage touched by blue dragons—manifests as crackling lightning and an innate resilience that has saved his life more than once.

Behind his noble bearing lies a sharp tactical mind and a willingness to do what must be done. Some might call him calculating; he prefers "practical."

🧠 Psychological Profile

There is a deliberate contradiction at the center of Semaj Dra'hcir, and he knows it. He moves through the world with the bearing of someone who has decided, consciously and at great personal cost, to invest in other people — not because it is easy for him, but precisely because it is not. When the Dendrar children were starving in the Redbrand dungeons, Semaj did not make a speech. He quietly passed food through the bars and then used Message to warn the party, letting the action speak for itself. That restraint is characteristic. He is suspicious of displays, his own included.

He thinks in terms of leverage, positioning, and information — the trained habits of a long-lived mind accustomed to outlasting threats rather than meeting them head-on. He scans a room before committing to it. He coordinates before charging. When Glass Staff fled through the Trezendar ruins, Semaj's first thought was not rage but rapid reassessment: what spells does this man have, what is his most likely path, where can Kaelia and Aster cut off his routes? That he also landed a Chromatic Orb and watched the man burn is almost a secondary fact. He wanted the threat ended efficiently. The violence was incidental.

What is not incidental is Larry. The wolf is the most honest part of Semaj's emotional life, the one place where he does not check himself. When Reidoth spoke with Larry inside the Thundertree cottage and the wolf told the druid that Semaj's group had saved him, kept him close instead of chasing him off — Semaj's only response was "good boy, Larry." But the look that came with it, directed at a wolf who could not understand pride, contained everything Semaj does not say out loud about why he is here at all.

And then there is Ruxithid. Semaj has been sitting with a name in his skull for weeks, something alien and patient that reached into his thoughts in Glass Staff's workshop and has not fully let go. His astral meditation at the campfire — the quiet exchange with Kaelia about aberrant powers and long games, the way he holds that particular thread of dread apart from the rest — this is a man who has decided not to be afraid of something he knows he should be afraid of. He will fight when the moment comes. Until then, he watches.

✨ Features & Traits

Draconic Resilience
Blue dragon bloodline toughens the skin. Unarmored AC equals 13 + DEX modifier, and max HP increases by 1 per sorcerer level.
Dragon Ancestor (Blue Dragon)
Lightning damage affinity. Proficiency bonus is doubled for Charisma checks made to interact with blue dragons.
Metamagic
Quickened Spell (2 SP: change casting time to bonus action) and Twinned Spell (1 SP per spell level: target a second creature with the same spell).
Starlight Step
As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Usable twice per long rest.
Astral Trance
Requires no sleep — meditates for 4 hours instead. Magic cannot put Semaj to sleep. Gains a temporary skill and weapon/tool proficiency on waking.
