📖 Backstory

Semaj Dra'hcir's story begins in the celestial realms of Astelloria, a known haven for astral elves legendary for their connection to the stars and the arcane energies of the cosmos. Born into the Dra'hcir family, whose name was synonymous with wealth and nobility, Semaj was groomed to uphold a facade of opulence. Without his knowledge, and behind the grandeur of public appearances, the Dra'hcir estate was crumbling under a mountain of debts, secretly owed to a cabal of interdimensional traders known for their merciless, and sometimes downright brutal, business dealings.

From a young age, Semaj was continuously showered with praise while adorned in garments that appeared to define the splendor of true royalty — cloaks that shimmered, but with the false glint of gold; tunics studded with counterfeit gems. This deception was a necessary veneer to mask the family's financial woes and to maintain their social standing among the elite within the highest societal circles of Astelloria.

However, despite the lies woven into the very fabric of his upbringing, Semaj surprisingly developed a reputation for his unwavering loyalty and bravery. Known to his elvish friends as a lovable trickster, he possessed a razor-sharp wit and a readiness to plunge into adventures (or misadventures), often blurring the line between the two.

His turning point came on his 100th birthday. Confronted with the truth about his family's legacy — a birthright fabricated on deceit and less-than-reputable dealings — Semaj decided to leave Astelloria. His departure was not only a quest to forge his own path and build a genuine legacy, but also a decision to escape the inevitable fallout when his family's creditors would come calling.

🧠 Psychological Profile

Semaj Dra'hcir came up inside a lie. The Dra'hcir name, the fine clothes, the reverence he was shown on the Astral Plane — all of it was performance sustained by debt, maintained with counterfeit gems and borrowed credibility until he turned one hundred and the floor fell out. He is not, as a result, a person who trusts surfaces. He knows better than almost anyone that what a thing appears to be and what it is are often violently unrelated, because the most comforting thing in his life turned out to be the fiction. What the campaign keeps testing is whether a man raised inside a performance can learn to offer something genuine — and whether he believes it will hold.

The transcript evidence says he is trying. When the Dendrar boy pressed himself against the bars in that Redbrand dungeon, Semaj did not make a speech. He passed food through the bars in silence, told the child to stay quiet, and used Message to alert the rest of the party rather than say a word aloud. There is no audience for that. It is not a gesture designed to be seen. For someone who grew up in a family where every action was calculated for its impressional value, doing a thing without theater is not nothing — it is, arguably, the whole project. He holds the Signet Ring of House Dra'hcir in his pack like evidence he is not quite ready to interpret.

His mind moves in the shape his upbringing carved: leverage, information, positioning. He scanned Glasstaff's workshop before committing to it. He coordinated the party's angles during the chase through Trezendar. When Ruxithid's name surfaced in the astral meditation outside Thundertree, he held it close for weeks before sharing it with Kaelia at the campfire — and even then he shared only when she offered something first, each of them offering information carefully, in stages, like people who have spent a long time not being able to afford the cost of being wrong. This is his native language. He simply learned to speak it before he learned to speak anything else.

Larry is the exception to all of it. When Reidoth spoke with Larry inside the Thundertree cottage and the wolf reported that the group had dragged him out of trouble, fed him, kept him close — Semaj's only response was "good boy, Larry," delivered to an animal who could not understand the weight of it. But the weight is there. A wolf who was chained in the dark and is now here, beside him, by choice — that is what Semaj is trying to build. Not the Dra'hcir name restored. Something that, when examined closely, turns out to actually be what it looks like.

✨ Features & Traits

Draconic Resilience
Blue dragon bloodline toughens the skin. Unarmored AC equals 13 + DEX modifier, and max HP increases by 1 per sorcerer level.
Dragon Ancestor (Blue Dragon)
Lightning damage affinity. Proficiency bonus is doubled for Charisma checks made to interact with blue dragons.
Metamagic
Quickened Spell (2 SP: change casting time to bonus action) and Twinned Spell (1 SP per spell level: target a second creature with the same spell).
Starlight Step
As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Usable twice per long rest.
Astral Trance
Requires no sleep — meditates for 4 hours instead. Magic cannot put Semaj to sleep. Gains a temporary skill and weapon/tool proficiency on waking.
Sorcerous Versatility
When reaching a level that grants an ASI or feat, Semaj may swap one known cantrip or one Metamagic option for another available choice.
Magic Initiate (Cleric) — Feat
Grants two cleric cantrips (Guidance and Toll the Dead) and one 1st-level cleric spell (Healing Word), castable once per long rest without expending a spell slot.
