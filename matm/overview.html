The Story So Far

In the year 1491, a group of strangers gathered at the Driftwood Tavern in Neverwinter, drawn together by fate—or perhaps by the scheming of forces beyond their understanding. The dwarf Gundren Rockseeker hired them for a simple task: escort supplies to the frontier town of Phandalin.

But nothing is simple when ancient magic stirs. Gundren claimed to have found the lost Wave Echo Cave, home to the legendary Forge of Spells. Before they could learn more, he was captured by goblins working for a mysterious figure known only as the Black Spider.

The party fought through goblin caves, stared down wolves by firelight, and waged war against the Redbrands who had terrorized Phandalin. They've made allies, lost friends, and stumbled upon darker mysteries—including the whispered name of Ruxithid, a psychic tyrant from beyond the world.

Now, at level 4, they stand on the precipice of something greater. Venomfang is dead, and so is the cult that came to worship him — Favric fell to Beldan's hammer mid-sermon, his dying words a prophecy: "You will kill them all." But Valrex still carries Venomfang's curse, one cultist escaped to tell the tale, and the dragon's unclaimed horde whispers from the tower. The Forge of Spells awaits. The Black Spider hunts. And fire answers to those who refuse to kneel.
