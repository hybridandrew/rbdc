What Lies Within

Barovia is not a place you choose to visit. The mists of the Ethereal Plane swallow travelers whole and deposit them here — a land of perpetual twilight, ancient evil, and no exit. Strahd von Zarovich has ruled this domain for centuries, a vampire lord whose boredom and obsession have calcified into something indistinguishable from the landscape itself.
